### SECTION 6 — Active Offers / Promotions

**Layout:** Horizontal scrolling cards with a subtle shimmer/gradient border animation.

**Use Magic UI's `ShimmerButton` and `BorderBeam`** components:
- Shimmer Button: https://magicui.design/docs/components/shimmer-button
- Border Beam: https://magicui.design/docs/components/border-beam

These are directly compatible with shadcn/ui and Tailwind.

### SECTION 7 — Testimonials

**Layout:** Auto-scrolling marquee (infinite horizontal scroll loop), dark cards, star rating, customer name.

**Use Magic UI's `Marquee` component:**
https://magicui.design/docs/components/marquee

---

### SECTION 8 — CTA / Book Now Banner

Full-width section with a blurred background image of the shop, a centered booking prompt, and a large button. Add a pulsing glow ring animation around the button using Tailwind `animate-ping`.

---

### SECTION 9 — Footer

Dark, minimal. Logo + navigation links + social media + WhatsApp number + copyright. The floating WhatsApp button persists above the footer as a fixed element.
